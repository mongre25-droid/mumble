Private by architecture

Your voice never leaves your device.

Not a setting — it's how Mumble is built. Speech-to-text runs on your machine. Only finished text can ever reach a provider, and only if you opt in with your own key.

  • Audio stays local — transcribed on-device, never uploaded.
  • No built-in key — every key is yours, stored only on your machine.
  • Works fully offline — turn Pro Mode off and nothing touches the network.
