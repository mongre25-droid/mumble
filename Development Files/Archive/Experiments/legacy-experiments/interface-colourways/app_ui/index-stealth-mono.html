What's inside Mumble

The Deck

Your local workspace — every transcript, clipboard item and saved prompt in one searchable place. Tick items, pick a preset or Smart Mode, and Go to reshape them. Opens with Ctrl + Alt + D.

Reader Mode

Have any document read aloud in a natural AI voice, with each word highlighted as it's spoken. Your library remembers where you left off.

Clean, plain text. Just talk — Mumble cleans up your speech and automatically shapes it into clean, punctuated text. Prompt is the one thing you switch on yourself (on the floating island, or right here) when you want a polished AI prompt. Convert now lives in the Deck.

AI Provider

Pro Mode ON: your transcribed text is sent to your AI provider for cleanup, formatting, and Smart Modes (email, prompt shaping). Audio never leaves your device.

Pro Mode OFF: Mumble uses offline, rule-based formatting — no network required, everything stays local. Smart Modes need Pro Mode + a valid key, or an on-device model (below).

On-device AI · fully offline

checking…

Shape Smart Modes entirely on your machine — no key, no network. Drop a small .gguf model into your models folder and Mumble runs prompts, emails and replies through the bundled on-device engine. With no model installed, offline shaping falls back to the faithful rule-based formatter — nothing breaks either way.

By default transcription runs entirely on-device with faster-whisper — your audio never leaves the machine. (An optional Cloud mode under Transcription can send audio to a hosted service for extra speed.)

Transcription

Transcription engine
Local transcription runs entirely on your device. Your audio never leaves this machine — it works offline, it's private by default, and nothing is ever uploaded. Recommended for most users.
Cloud transcription uploads each audio clip to a low-latency hosted Whisper service — faster on older hardware, but your audio is sent to the provider you choose. Turn this on only if you're comfortable with that privacy trade-off. If a request fails, Mumble silently falls back to local. Network required.

Hardware & model

Mumble runs faster-whisper speech models on your device. Bigger models are more accurate but need more RAM and CPU — Mumble picks the right one for your PC automatically. If a model fails to load, Mumble falls back to a safe default so transcription always works. GPU acceleration (CUDA) is used automatically when available; otherwise everything runs efficiently on CPU.

Your PC
Languages

Mumble picks the right on-device speech model for your PC and language — you don’t need to choose one. Turn off “English only” if you dictate in other languages.

Advanced — pick the exact model
Accuracy model

Higher accuracy tiers increase local hardware strain and extend transcription processing time.

Language

Bigger models are more accurate but slower to load and run. Everything runs locally on your CPU (or GPU if available). Languages other than English need a multilingual model — the “English only” models ignore the language setting.

⚠ Maximum Accuracy uses a large model (≈1.5 GB+) and is much slower. Not recommended unless you have a high-end CPU.

AI provider
  1. Click the button above to open your provider's API keys page
  2. Sign in (Google works on Cerebras)

  4. Copy the key (starts with csk- or sk-or-)
  5. Paste it below and hit Save & Test
