============================================================
  MUMBLE  -  private voice-to-text for macOS
  Talk, and it types - in any app. Your audio stays on your Mac.
============================================================

INSTALL  (one time)

  1) Move this "MacMumble" folder somewhere permanent first
     (e.g. your Documents or Applications folder). Don't run it
     from inside the .zip, and don't move it after installing.

  2) Open  "Install Mumble.command".
     macOS protects files from the internet, so the FIRST time:
        -> Right-click (or Control-click) "Install Mumble.command"
           -> choose "Open" -> click "Open" again.
     (If it says "permission denied" or won't run, see "If it won't
      open" at the bottom.)

  3) Let it finish (a few minutes the first time - it needs the
     internet to download what it needs).

  4) Grant the two permissions macOS asks for - this is required:
        -> Microphone     (so it can hear you)
        -> Accessibility  (so the hotkey works and it can type for you)
     You can also set these in
        System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Microphone / Accessibility
     (turn the switch ON for "python" / "Terminal").

  Mumble then lives in your MENU BAR (top-right of the screen) and
  starts automatically every time you log in. A guide opens in your
  browser the first time.


HOW TO USE IT

  - Click into any text box (browser, Notes, Mail, chat...).
  - Press   Control + Option + D .   Speak.   Press it again.
  - Your words are typed where the cursor is.

  Click the menu-bar icon any time for the Prompt toggle, Deck, and settings.
     Prompt toggle -> shapes your next dictation into a detailed AI prompt
     Deck           -> runs Email, List, Search, Reply, and other actions
  Re-paste your last dictation anywhere with  Control + Option + V .

  Local transcription keeps audio on your Mac. Optional Cloud transcription
  sends each audio clip to the provider you select. Pro Mode sends transcript
  text to your AI provider for polishing. Turn cloud features off to stay offline.


REQUIREMENTS
  - macOS 12 or newer, and internet for the one-time setup.

TO REMOVE IT
  - Open "Uninstall Mumble.command" the same way.


IF IT WON'T OPEN (permission denied / unidentified developer)
  Open the Terminal app (press Cmd+Space, type Terminal, press Enter),
  then type  bash  followed by a space, DRAG the "Install Mumble.command"
  file into the Terminal window, and press Enter. That runs it directly.
